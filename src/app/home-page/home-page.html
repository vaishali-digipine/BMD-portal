How It Works

Complete your application in just a few easy steps and access government certificate services from anywhere.

01.

Registration

Verify your Aadhaar details to create a secure account on the portal. Once registered, you can access all certificate services and manage your applications from a single dashboard.

02.

Verification

Choose the required certificate service, fill out the application form, and upload the necessary documents. Select a convenient date and time slot for online verification with an authorized clerk.

03.

Download Certificate

Attend the scheduled video verification and complete the document validation process. After approval, your certificate will be issued digitally and made available for download through the portal.
